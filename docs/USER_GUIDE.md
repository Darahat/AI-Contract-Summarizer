# ClauseWise User Guide

Welcome to ClauseWise! This guide will help you get started with analyzing your contracts.

## Getting Started

### First Time Setup

1. **Install the App**
   - Download from App Store / Google Play (when available)
   - Or run from source (see README)

2. **Configure API Key**
   - Open the app
   - Tap on Settings (gear icon)
   - Enter your OpenAI API key
   - Save

3. **You're Ready!**
   - Start uploading contracts

## Uploading Your First Contract

1. **Tap the Upload Button**
   - On the home screen, tap "Upload Contract"
   - Or use the floating action button

2. **Select a Document**
   - Choose a file from your device
   - Supported formats: PDF, DOC, DOCX, TXT
   - Maximum size: 10 MB

3. **Wait for Analysis**
   - The document is encrypted and stored
   - AI analyzes the contract (usually 30-60 seconds)
   - You'll see a notification when complete

## Understanding Your Analysis

### Contract Summary

The summary provides a high-level overview:
- **Main Parties**: Who are the contracting parties?
- **Purpose**: What is this agreement about?
- **Key Obligations**: What are the main responsibilities?
- **Payment Terms**: How and when are payments made?
- **Duration**: How long does the contract last?
- **Important Deadlines**: Key dates to remember

### Risk Detection

ClauseWise identifies three types of risks:

#### 🔴 Payment Risks
Issues related to how and when you get paid:
- Unclear payment schedules
- Unfavorable payment terms
- Missing payment protection
- Delayed payment clauses

**Example Risk:**
```
"Payment due 90 days after invoice"
Risk: Long payment delay could impact cash flow
Recommendation: Negotiate for Net 30 or Net 45 terms
```

#### ⚠️ Liability Risks
Issues that could expose you to legal liability:
- Unlimited liability clauses
- Indemnification requirements
- Insurance obligations
- Warranty provisions

**Example Risk:**
```
"Contractor assumes all liability for any damages"
Risk: Unlimited liability exposure
Recommendation: Add liability cap (e.g., contract value)
```

#### 💼 Intellectual Property Risks
Issues related to IP ownership and rights:
- IP ownership ambiguity
- Work-for-hire implications
- Usage rights concerns
- IP transfer clauses

**Example Risk:**
```
"All work product becomes client property"
Risk: You lose all rights to your work
Recommendation: Retain portfolio/showcase rights
```

### Risk Levels

Each risk is categorized:
- **🟢 Low**: Minor concern, good to be aware
- **🟡 Medium**: Should be addressed before signing
- **🟠 High**: Significant risk, negotiate changes
- **🔴 Critical**: Major red flag, seek legal advice

## Tips & Best Practices

### Before Uploading

1. **Review the Contract**
   - Read through it first
   - Note any specific concerns
   - Highlight confusing sections

2. **Check File Format**
   - Ensure it's PDF, DOC, DOCX, or TXT
   - Under 10 MB in size
   - Text is selectable (not scanned image)

3. **Verify API Key**
   - Make sure you have credits
   - Check key is active
   - Monitor usage costs

### Interpreting Results

1. **Focus on High-Risk Items**
   - Address critical and high risks first
   - Medium risks are important but negotiable
   - Low risks are good to know

2. **Don't Ignore Context**
   - AI provides guidance, not legal advice
   - Consider your specific situation
   - Industry standards vary

3. **When in Doubt, Ask**
   - Consult a lawyer for critical contracts
   - Use ClauseWise to identify issues first
   - Saves time and money on legal review

### Security Best Practices

1. **Protect Your Device**
   - Use screen lock
   - Keep OS updated
   - Don't share device

2. **Manage API Keys**
   - Don't share with anyone
   - Rotate periodically
   - Monitor usage

3. **Be Selective**
   - Don't upload extremely sensitive docs
   - Remember docs are sent to AI for analysis
   - Review AI provider privacy policies

## Troubleshooting

### "Monthly Limit Reached"
- You've used your free 2 documents
- Wait until next month
- Or upgrade to Pro for unlimited

### "API Key Invalid"
- Check key is entered correctly
- Verify key hasn't been revoked
- Ensure billing is set up

### "Analysis Failed"
- Check internet connection
- Verify API credits available
- Try uploading again
- Contact support if persists

### "File Too Large"
- Maximum size is 10 MB
- Compress PDF
- Split large document
- Extract relevant sections

### Slow Processing
- Complex contracts take longer
- Check internet speed
- Try during off-peak hours
- Upgrade internet connection

## FAQs

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes! All documents are encrypted with AES and stored locally on your device.

**Q: Do you store my contracts?**
A: No, contracts are only stored locally on your device. They're sent temporarily to AI providers for analysis only.

**Q: Can I use this for legal advice?**
A: No, ClauseWise provides information and identifies potential issues, but it's not a substitute for legal advice.

**Q: What if I find an issue?**
A: Use the analysis to inform your negotiations or consult a lawyer for specific concerns.

**Q: How accurate is the AI?**
A: GPT-4 is highly capable, but always review results and use your judgment.

**Q: Can I export my analyses?**
A: Export feature coming soon in Pro plan.

**Q: What happens if I cancel Pro?**
A: You'll revert to the free plan (2 docs/month) at the end of your billing period.
